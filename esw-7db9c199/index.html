What is this?

ES-World is a heterogeneous multi-agent simulation environment: UAVs, quadruped robots, people and robots move through 5 common small scenes, and what each of them sees, hears and receives obeys physics (occlusion, sound propagation, radio links). Place them on the map, pick a location, and the result appears on the right at once; or click "First-person walk-through" to stand in the scene and walk around. Start with the "Quick examples" below.
